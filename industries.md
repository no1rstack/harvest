Add several domains that many OSINT platforms don't emphasize because they become very powerful when correlated.

**Critical Infrastructure**

Instead of treating infrastructure as a single topic, separate it into:

* Electric grid
* Oil & gas
* Water
* Telecommunications
* Transportation
* Financial infrastructure
* Cloud infrastructure
* Space infrastructure

**Industrial Base**

Track:

* Manufacturers
* Suppliers
* Production capacity
* Factory openings/closures
* Industrial investments
* Labor shortages
* Raw materials

This supports your "Industrial Base" editorial.

**Science & Technology**

Separate by discipline:

* Artificial Intelligence
* Quantum
* Biotechnology
* Nuclear
* Semiconductors
* Robotics
* Materials science
* Chemistry
* Physics
* Astronomy
* Oceanography

**Government Operations**

Instead of only contracts:

* Budgets
* Hearings
* Rulemaking
* Inspectors General
* GAO reports
* OMB guidance
* Agency strategic plans
* Performance metrics

**Influence & Governance**

Track:

* Lobbying
* Campaign finance
* Corporate ownership
* Nonprofits
* Think tanks
* International organizations
* Public-private partnerships
